Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Overview

ToolBox Pro ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. All tools run entirely in your browser - no data is sent to our servers.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any data you input into our tools. All processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.

Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to save your preferences (theme, premium status) and anonymous usage counts. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Plausible or Simple Analytics) to understand general usage patterns. These do not use cookies or track individual users.

Advertising

Free tier users may see non-personalized advertisements. PRO users see no advertisements. We do not share personal data with advertisers.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party services we may integrate are:

  • Payment processor (Stripe) for PRO subscriptions - subject to their privacy policy
  • Privacy-respecting analytics - no personal data collected

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at support@toolboxpro.dev
